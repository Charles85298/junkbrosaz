Clear the space. Keep your weekend.

Junk piles rarely start as a big project. A few boxes stay in the garage. An old chair gets pushed to the side. Then a move, remodel or cleanup turns it into more than you want to haul yourself. AZ Junk Brothers helps homeowners and businesses in Surprise clear unwanted items without the lifting, loading and dump runs.

What we can help remove

We handle common household junk, furniture, appliances, garage clutter, yard debris and accepted remodeling debris. Have several types of items? That is fine. Show us everything you want gone and we can look at the job as a whole. If you are unsure about an item, ask us before we arrive.

You do not have to move it to the curb

Leave the heavy work to us. Point out what goes and our crew handles the carrying and loading. Tell us about stairs, narrow hallways, gates or other access issues when you contact us. A few photos can help us understand the amount of material and give you a better idea of what the job involves.

Useful for more than junk

We often help when someone is moving, downsizing, cleaning out a rental, preparing a home for sale or finally getting the garage back. We also help with estate cleanouts, office cleanups and debris left from home projects. The goal is simple: remove what you no longer need and leave you with usable space again.
