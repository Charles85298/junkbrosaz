Old appliances are heavy. We make the removal simple.

A refrigerator, washer or dryer can take up valuable space long after it stops being useful. Moving one is also harder than it looks. AZ Junk Brothers handles the lifting, loading and haul-away for accepted appliances in Surprise and nearby West Valley communities.

Common appliance pickups

We can discuss refrigerators, washers, dryers, dishwashers, ranges and other household appliances we are able to accept. If you also have furniture, boxes or garage clutter, tell us. Combining items into one cleanup may be easier than scheduling separate pickups.

A little preparation helps

Tell us where the appliance is located and whether we will deal with stairs, tight doors or gates. Appliances should be disconnected from water, gas and electrical connections before removal. If you are not sure what needs to be done, ask when you request your estimate.

Helpful after upgrades, moves and rental turnovers

New appliance delivery does not always include removing everything you want gone. We help homeowners, landlords and property managers clear old appliances after upgrades, moves and property cleanouts.
