Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect information when you visit our website or request service.

Effective and last updated: August 21, 2026

AZ Junk Brothers LLC ("AZ Junk Brothers," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect and share information when you visit junkbrosaz.com, contact us, request an estimate, or use our junk removal and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you request an estimate or service, submit a contact form, call or text us, send us an email, schedule service, or provide information needed to complete a job.

This information may include your name, phone number, email address, service address, city or ZIP code, details about items or materials you need removed, photos you provide for an estimate, preferred service date or time, and other information you choose to provide.

2. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through standard website technologies. This may include your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, approximate location, referring website or search engine, visit date and time, and website interaction information.

We may use cookies, analytics services and similar technologies to understand how visitors use our website and to improve its performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use information we collect to provide junk removal and related services, respond to questions and estimate requests, schedule and manage appointments, contact you regarding requested services, provide customer support, improve our website and services, understand website usage, maintain business records, prevent fraud or abuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Phone Calls and Text Messages

If you provide your phone number, we may use it to communicate with you about estimates, appointments, scheduling, service updates and other matters related to your request. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Providing your phone number does not authorize us to sell or share your number with third parties for their independent marketing purposes. You may ask us to stop text messages at any time by replying STOP or contacting us directly.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, measure website traffic and understand how visitors interact with the site. We may use third-party analytics or advertising services, including Google services, which may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6. How We Share Information

We may share information with service providers that help us operate our business and website, such as website hosting providers, form or email providers, analytics providers, scheduling or customer-management services, payment processors when applicable, and professional advisers.

These providers may receive information only as reasonably necessary to perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect our rights, customers, business or property.

7. Website Forms

Information submitted through our estimate form may be processed by a third-party form service so that your request can be delivered to us. Do not submit sensitive financial, medical, government identification or other information that is not necessary for a junk removal estimate.

8. Payment Information

If electronic payments are offered, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider. AZ Junk Brothers does not intentionally store complete credit or debit card numbers on this website unless specifically stated when payment information is collected.

9. Photos

Customers may provide photos of junk, furniture, construction debris, property areas or other materials to help us evaluate a job or provide an estimate. We use these photos for legitimate business purposes related to your request.

We will not intentionally publish identifiable customer photos for advertising or promotional purposes without appropriate permission.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss or disclosure. However, no website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and protect legitimate business interests. Information that is no longer reasonably necessary may be deleted or securely disposed of.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services. AZ Junk Brothers is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites. Review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults seeking junk removal and related services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

14. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information we have about you, request corrections, request deletion when appropriate, opt out of certain communications, or ask how your information is used. Some information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, contractual or legitimate business purposes.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our website, services, technology or legal requirements. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date on this page will be revised.
