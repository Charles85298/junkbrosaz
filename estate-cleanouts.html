Practical help during a big cleanout

An estate or downsizing project can involve years of belongings and a lot of decisions. The hauling should be the simple part. AZ Junk Brothers helps families remove furniture, household items and other accepted contents after you decide what stays and what goes.

You stay in control

We do not decide what should leave the property. You do. Once the removal items are identified, our crew handles the lifting, loading and haul-away. For a larger home or several rooms, photos and a short conversation can help us understand the scope before scheduling.

More than furniture

Estate cleanouts may include mattresses, boxes, garage contents, appliances, patio items and general household clutter. If there are several areas of the property to clear, tell us about all of them so we can plan the job as one project.

Helpful for downsizing and preparing a home

We can help when a family member is moving, a home is being prepared for sale, a rental needs to be cleared or an estate is being settled. Some cleanouts happen all at once. Others happen in stages. We can work with the part that is ready.

Start with what you know needs to go

Call or text (602) 672-0772 when you are ready. Describe the rooms or items you want removed and send photos if that is easier. We will review the job with you and provide a free estimate before work begins.
