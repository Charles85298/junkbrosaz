Get your garage back

Garages have a way of becoming the place where everything goes “for now.” Boxes stack up. Old furniture gets pushed against a wall. Leftovers from projects fill the corners. Before long, there is no room for the car. AZ Junk Brothers can help clear the items you no longer want so the garage works for you again.

You decide what stays

A garage cleanout does not mean everything has to go. Separate the things you want to keep or simply point out what we should remove. We can haul accepted household clutter, furniture, shelving, appliances, outdoor items and other unwanted material.

No need to stage everything at the curb

Our crew handles the lifting and loading. For a larger garage, send a few photos so we can see the amount of material before scheduling. That makes it easier to understand the scope and plan the job.

Make room for the next project

A clean garage can give you space to park, organize storage, build a workshop or prepare for a move. We also help homeowners, landlords and property managers clear garages before a sale or rental turnover.

Start with the part that bothers you most

You do not have to solve the whole garage in one day. If one section or a few large items are the problem, start there. Call or text (602) 672-0772. Tell us what you want gone and we will provide a free estimate before the work begins.
