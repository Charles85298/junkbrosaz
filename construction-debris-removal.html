Keep the project moving

A remodel can fill a garage or driveway with debris faster than expected. Flooring, cabinets, drywall and lumber get in the way of the next phase of work. AZ Junk Brothers hauls accepted construction and remodeling debris for homeowners, landlords and contractors in Surprise and the West Valley.

Debris from everyday home projects

We can discuss material from flooring replacement, cabinet removal, drywall work, room updates and other renovation projects. Construction debris can be dense and heavy, so tell us what the material is and roughly how much you have. If you are unsure whether we can accept something, ask before scheduling.

Small remodels create big piles too

You do not need a commercial construction site to need hauling help. A bathroom remodel, new flooring, rental turnover or weekend demolition can create more debris than a household vehicle can reasonably handle.

Plan removal around the work

Clearing debris gives installers and trades more room and helps keep the work area manageable. Contact us before the project if you know what will be removed, or send photos once the pile is ready.
