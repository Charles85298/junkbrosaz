Heavy furniture does not need to become your weekend project

A couch can look easy to move until it reaches a doorway. Add stairs, a tight hallway or Arizona heat and the job gets old fast. AZ Junk Brothers removes bulky furniture from homes, apartments, rentals and businesses in Surprise and across the West Valley.

One piece or a whole room

We can help with couches, sectionals, chairs, tables, desks, mattresses, bed frames, dressers, shelving, patio furniture and other accepted pieces. Replacing several rooms of furniture? Cleaning out a rental? Tell us about the full job so we can plan the pickup around what you actually need.

We handle the lifting

You do not need to drag everything outside first. Show us what is going and our crew handles the carrying and loading. Let us know about stairs, elevators, gates or especially heavy pieces. Photos are useful when an item is oversized or access is tight.

Good timing makes a move easier

Furniture removal often happens before new furniture arrives, during a move, after a remodel or while helping a family member downsize. Getting the old pieces out first gives you room to work and one less thing to manage.

Ready to make some room?
